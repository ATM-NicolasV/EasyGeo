<product_requirements>
The application aims to simplify understanding complex topics in politics, geopolitics, and conspiracy theories for web and mobile users. Key functionalities include:
-   **Multi-source Information Synthesis:** Aggregating articles and analyses from diverse media sources (e.g., BFM Business, Blast, Le Monde).
-   **AI Analysis & Cross-referencing:** Utilizing an open-source AI (specifically Anthropic Claude) to analyze, cross-reference, and verify information, extracting corroborated facts while ignoring opinions.
-   **Neutral Presentation:** Generating entirely neutral synthesis articles focused solely on verified facts, with each piece of information linked to its source.
-   **Complex Term De-vulgarization:** An interactive glossary providing simple definitions for complex terms (e.g., PIB, OTAN) with links to detailed explanations.
-   **Personalization & Contribution:** Users can add supplementary information sources for AI analysis.
-   **Reliability Scoring System:** Evaluating information correlation across sources to assign a reliability index (e.g., higher score for information cited by multiple reliable sources).
-   **Topic Evolution History:** Tracking how facts and narratives evolve over time across sources.
-   **For and Against Mode:** Presenting main arguments for and against a subject, citing sources, without taking sides.
-   **Simplified News Feed:** Daily/weekly summary of major political/geopolitical events.
The application is to be built using React.
</product_requirements>
